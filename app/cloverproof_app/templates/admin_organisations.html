What joining means

Joining gives you access to that organisation's farmers, fields, plans and users. As a superadmin member you can also approve, edit and reject its plans. Those reviews are recorded as superadmin reviews, not agronomist reviews, and never enter the calibration data.

Every join is recorded under your account in the audit log. This page cannot remove you again.
