## SR&ED record-keeping checklist for S3

Mark all as "confirm with the accountant / SR&ED preparer". Source for the rate and expenditure-limit facts: ITC policy page above.

1. **Per-project register.** One row per technical question, e.g. "Can method X predict N rate for crop Y without a soil test within a confidence band?", with start and end dates.
2. **State the uncertainty at the start.** Dated note (in `docs/`, committed) of what was unknown, why standard practice and published methods did not answer it, and the hypothesis. Write it before the work, not after.
3. **Record each experiment.** Hypothesis, method tried, inputs, result, why it failed or worked, next iteration. Failed approaches matter. ADRs and golden-test cases are good evidence; keep them commit-linked.
4. **Preserve the calibration data.** Engine output vs agronomist final number with reason, per recommendation (already a hard rule in CLAUDE.md). This is the core evidence of systematic investigation.
5. **Separate routine from R&D.** Tag work: R&D (nutrient method development, confidence-rule design, calibration analysis) vs routine (CRUD, auth, PDF layout, HTMX UI, deployment). Do not claim the routine part.
6. **Time tracking.** Weekly time log per person (founder, contractors) by project and by R&D vs non-R&D tag, kept at the time, not reconstructed. The agronomist `minutes_spent` metric is a different thing; do not use it as payroll evidence.
7. **Who did the work.** Employment or contract status, T4 vs invoice, dates, location of work (Canada), and written contractor agreements stating who owns the IP. Whether the founder's salary is claimable depends on payroll status: ask the accountant.
8. **Costs.** Keep invoices for contractors, cloud and compute used for experiments, and any third-party agronomy or lab work, separately from general overhead.
9. **Git hygiene.** Commits referencing the experiment ID; keep branch history for experiment work; do not squash away the failed attempts.
10. **Entity and funding trail.** Record which legal entity paid for each piece of work, and any grants or assistance received (government assistance reduces the claim). Keep associated-corporation facts (Ashavid and VisaRoads) for the expenditure-limit agreement.
11. **Calendar.** Fiscal year-end, T2 filing date, and the SR&ED claim deadline: confirm the exact date with the accountant (not verified on a page this run).
12. **Pre-claim approval.** CRA landing page lists an optional pre-claim approval before work starts. Ask the accountant whether it makes sense; I could not open the detail page.
