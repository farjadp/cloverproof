**Rules followed.** Read-only research. Nobody was contacted, nothing submitted, no accounts created. Contact routes are application forms or general inboxes only. "unknown" = not stated on the organisation's own site on 2026-11-17; no cheque size, stage or portfolio name is invented. **Our position (for fit scoring).** Pre-revenue, pre-seed, software-only, vendor-neutral. Pilot with 5–10 Ontario winter wheat farms Feb–Apr 2027; pilot season May–Oct 2027. No human agronomist named yet; no partnerships, no endorsements, no measured traction. Raising entity undecided (Ashavid Inc. vs a new CloverProof corporation).

**Fit scale.** H = stage, geography and thesis match a pre-seed Ontario ag-software company now or right after the pilot. M = one of those is off (stage later, geography, or thesis broad). L = mismatch or a conflict that likely outweighs the money.

**Vendor-neutrality conflict rule.** Money or LP ties to fertilizer / crop-input sellers (e.g. Nutrien, Mosaic, CHS, GROWMARK) are flagged. Taking such money is not forbidden, but it undercuts the core claim ("vendor-neutral"); `cso` should decide a policy before any term sheet.
