Trade-offs the radar reveals:

1. **SR&ED follows the payer.** The claim belongs to the corporation that incurred the expenditures. Work the Ashavid team does during S1-S5 (Nov 2026 to Jan 2027) is Ashavid's expenditure. Moving the product into a new corp later means moving IP and splitting records. **Confirm with the accountant before the next payroll or contractor invoice.**
2. **Association probably applies either way.** Associated CCPCs share one expenditure limit and must file the allocation agreement (T2SCH49) or the limit is treated as nil (CRA ITC policy page). The limit is $6 million for tax years beginning after 15 Dec 2024, so sharing is not a practical constraint at this scale. Whether a new corp controlled by the same person would be associated with Ashavid and VisaRoads: not on a page I read; confirm with the accountant.
3. **One-application-per-group rules.** FedDev allows one application per business or organization per intake across all its open programs (FedDev "When to apply" page). Whether a corporate group counts as one: not stated. CSJ counts "50 or fewer full-time employees"; how groups are counted: not stated.
4. **Separation vs history.** A new corp gives a clean IP, liability and fund-accounting boundary (useful if real farmer data and a possible human agronomist partner come in) but costs every history gate above and starts a new first fiscal year with no financial statements.
5. **VisaRoads Inc. (inc. 11 Dec 2023) passes the two-year age tests**, but it is a startup-mentorship company; its FTE count is UNKNOWN. Putting an agri-software product there to pass an age gate would misrepresent the business to the program. Do not do it for funding reasons. Challenge noted: an entity chosen to pass a gate is the kind of thing a program officer asks about.
6. **My recommendation (not a legal or tax opinion):** keep CloverProof in Ashavid Inc. until revenue exists, unless liability or investor reasons (not grant reasons) say otherwise. Revisit when a human agronomist, farmer data in Canada or an investor forces the question. **Confirm with the accountant / corporate lawyer.**

Facts that would change verdicts (answer in `company-facts.md`): CCPC status; T4 employees per company; whether Farjad is paid by T4 or dividends; fiscal year-end and whether Ashavid has filed or will file a first T2; Ashavid and Contivo revenue; prior government funding; Farjad's age (Futurpreneur 18-39); whether a student or graduate would be hired.

## 3. Programs

### 1. SR&ED federal investment tax credit (CRA)

- **Funds / amount:** Deduction against income and/or investment tax credit (ITC) on qualifying R&D done in Canada. Basic ITC 15 percent; enhanced 35 percent for CCPCs, up to the expenditure limit ($6 million for tax years beginning after 15 Dec 2024). CCPC refund: 100 percent of the ITC on current expenditures for qualifying CCPCs; non-CCPC corporations get a non-refundable ITC. Source: CRA ITC policy page, https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program/investment-tax-credit-policy.html (2026-11-17); "What are SR&ED tax incentives", .../what-are-sred-tax-incentives.html (2026-11-17).
- **Eligibility and fit:** Work must be in Canada and meet the technological-advancement and systematic-investigation tests ("What work is eligible", .../sred-eligibility.html, 2026-11-17). Fit depends on whether any CloverProof work is real R&D: see `docs/funding/sred-irap-readiness.md`. Government assistance (for example IRAP) reduces the ITC; provincial R&D credits reduce claimable expenditures ("What are SR&ED tax incentives").
- **Scenarios:** Ash: Check eligibility (CCPC status, who is paid, fiscal year UNKNOWN). New: Check eligibility; nothing claimable until it pays people. Both: confirm with accountant.
- **Intake:** Continuous. Reporting deadline for corporations is 12 months after the T2 due date (T2 is due 6 months after year-end), so about 18 months after year-end; CRA recommends filing at least 90 days before. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program/sred-claim/get-ready.html (2026-11-17).
- **Pre-claim approval (optional):** Evaluates planned work before it starts; open to CCPCs, other Canadian corporations and partnerships with gross business income under $25M and in good standing; up to 3 projects, each valid up to 3 years; applied for through My Business Account (Farjad). Page dated 2026-10-14. Sources: .../apply-pre-claim-approval.html and .../what-are-sred-tax-incentives.html (2026-11-17). Not stated: fees and processing time.
- **Matching:** None. **Documents:** T661 with the T2; records of work done, who, when and how expenditures were calculated (get-ready page).
- **Associated corporations:** share one limit; allocation agreement T2SCH49 or the limit is treated as nil (ITC policy page).
- **Next action (Farjad):** ask the accountant whether CloverProof salaries or contractors can be claimed, CCPC status, fiscal year, and whether to apply for pre-claim approval. Start the records in the readiness file now.
