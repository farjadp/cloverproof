## 0. How to use
- Placeholders in `{braces}` are filled per recipient. `[confirm]` marks a fact only Farjad Iranmehr can confirm; ship nothing with a `[confirm]` left in it.
- `{SITE_URL}` = the live website (preview today: https://website-production-a1991e11.up.railway.app). Always link to `/en`. Test each link once before sending.
- UTM codes carry no personal names. Keep the code-to-person map in the CRM, not in the link. Values below follow the conventions in `landing-tracking-brief.md` section 6.
- Sender block (every email), `{SENDER_BLOCK}`:
  `Farjad Iranmehr, CloverProof ([confirm] legal entity name), [confirm] mailing address, [confirm] reply-to email.`
- Opt-out line (every email), `{OPTOUT}`:
  `If you would rather not hear from me about this, reply "stop" and I will not write again.`
- Consent basis, per CASL operating rule (plan section 6, NOT LEGAL ADVICE, pending `legal-compliance-specialist`): one-to-one, personalised, relevant to the recipient's business role, sent to a publicly listed business address with no "do not email" notice, or to someone with an existing relationship. If neither applies: do not email. Log source and date.
- Reviewer wording (D4): "reviewed before it reaches you". Pilot terms free of charge are an assumption `[confirm]` (A1).
