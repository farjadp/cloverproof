## Accredited tests (same for every lab listed)

The page states every lab in the table is accredited for **pH, buffer pH, phosphorus (P), potassium (K), magnesium (Mg), Mn index, Zn index and nitrate-N** on Ontario soils. OMAFA also monitors (not accredited): Ca (ammonium acetate), soil organic matter (LOI), Cu (DTPA), S (PO4 extraction).
