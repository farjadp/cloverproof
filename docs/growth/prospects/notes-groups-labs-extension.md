# Groups, labs, extension: prospect notes (extends partner-shortlist.md)

Date: 2026-11-17. Nothing contacted. CSV: `orgs-groups-labs-extension.csv` (30 rows). No endorsement or partnership is claimed anywhere.

## Method
One fetch per page, no crawling, no logins. Contact channels copied from each org's own contact page. Where a page showed only a named person's address, it was not recorded as the channel (rows marked `unknown`). Search-engine snippets are labelled as such.

## Blocked / failed
- ontario.ca (lab list page and the OMAFA Agricultural Information Contact Centre page): HTTP 403 again. web.archive.org: blocked by the fetch tool. So the official accredited-lab list was NOT read. Lab accreditation is "unverified" for every lab; secondary hints are in each row's note.
- 404 or dead: oat-ato.ca (guessed), ontariograinfarmer.ca/contact, Ridgetown contact page, stratfordagri.ca/contact, actlabs.com/contact-us. SGS pages: 403 / socket closed.
- OFA, Christian Farmers: pages read, but no county federation contacts were captured (OFA routes via a form and MSR pages not fetched).

## Findings worth knowing
- OSCIA publishes regional role addresses (`<region>SCIA@ontariosoilcrop.org`) plus local presidents on its contact page; I recorded regional ones only, not individuals. No "no unsolicited email" notice seen. 11 regions; 9 recorded with a role address, 2 (Golden Horseshoe, St. Clair) lacked a usable one and are marked `unknown`.
- GFO publishes role emails (info, members, web, communications, advertising, agronomy manager). The agronomy manager's address is recorded as listed on the page: confirm it is meant for external inquiries. GFO's "Districts, Directors & Delegates" page was not fetched, so district committees are a gap.
- A&L's contact page lists a staff member with an agronomist title; not used.
- Field Crop News publishes no editor email, only a subscribe form and "Ask a Specialist".

## Coverage gaps
GFO district committees; OFA county federations; OSCIA local associations (listed on the contact page, not captured); Ontario Grain Farmer editorial address; OAFT (maybe inactive); the full OMAFA lab list (other labs may exist); U of Guelph OAC general inbox; Ridgetown inbox; OMAFA ACC email. CASL: no legal review; the bases are my reading, not advice. Retailer ownership of labs not checked.
