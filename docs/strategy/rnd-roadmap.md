## 0. Three facts that bound everything below
1. **Evidence = `agronomist_approved` units only.** `superadmin_approved` and `ai_reviewed` never count (calibration-loop.md, Update item 2; `.claude/agents/cp-agronomist.md`). Who the human agronomist is remains open (calibration-loop.md item 6, OQ-04). **Until a human agronomist is named, the R&D programme produces zero evidence.** This is the first gate, not a detail.
2. **The reference is one person's judgement, anchored on the engine number**, not crop response; there is no yield table (calibration-loop.md §0, gap G6).
3. **The 2027 season is anecdote-scale.** 5–10 farms cannot reach Tier 2 (n_plans ≥ 20 from ≥ 10 farms in one stratum); the expected output is "no proposal: below minimum sample" (calibration-loop.md §4.1).

## 1. Calibration science: from delta to pack update
The path is fixed by calibration-loop.md §6 and does not change by stage: evidence memo → `head-of-data` → CPO → **human agronomist decides value and source** → golden case first (`cp-engine`) → new pack version file (released versions never edited) → `cp-agronomy-guard` → golden report + replay (labelled in-sample) → prospective check. Never silent; never fitted values shipped without that approval (CLAUDE.md rules 4–6, 9). What changes per stage is the statistical method and **what the reference is**.

| Stage | Data | Reference | Method | Allowed output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S-A Descriptive** (2027) | Δ = final − engine per `recommendation_line`, `calibration_delta` view | Agronomist judgement (blind golden set and anchored tool set never pooled) | Exactly calibration-loop.md §4: bias, MAE, spread, Wilson intervals, farm-cluster bootstrap only from 10 farms, reason-code tagging | Tier 0 bugs, Tier 1 direction flags, questions to the agronomist. No values |
| **S-B Partial pooling** (2028, if ≥ 2 seasons or ≥ 20 farms) | Same, across seasons and strata | Agronomist judgement, ideally ≥ 2 human agronomists | Hierarchical model of Δ with farm and season random effects and stratum effects (previous crop, soil-test method, bound applied). Sparse strata borrow strength instead of being dropped; posterior intervals reported beside, **not instead of**, the §4.1 Tier 2 rule unless `head-of-data` amends calibration-loop.md. Hierarchical Bayesian models on on-farm N data have precedent (Kyveryga et al., Iowa strip trials, [AGRIS record](https://agris.fao.org/search/en/records/65de5e9f7c7033e84beac41d)) | Tier 2 memos in the largest stratum, if met; inter-rater disagreement as a new signal |
| **S-C Crop response** (2028 pilot strips → 2029) | Replicated N-rate strips on consenting farms + harvest yield (needs G6 solved and a yield record) | **Crop response**, not judgement | Per-trial response fit, then hierarchical pooling across trials. Field-scale randomized N-rate designs: DIFM on-farm precision experiments, 100+ trials in 8 US states and 3 other countries ([Illinois ACES](https://aces.illinois.edu/node/2436), search excerpt); Alberta network found the economic optimum below the producer rate in 6 of 10 N trials ([Olds College](https://oldscollege.ca/smart-farm-research/research-projects/smart-ag/current/on-farm-precision-experimentation.html)). Ontario precedent: Ridgetown winter wheat N rate × planting date study ([Ontario Grain Farmer, 2026-04-01](https://ontariograinfarmer.ca/2026/04/01/pushing-the-intensity-on-wheat-production/)) | Evidence a human agronomist can cite when validating a constant; the only stage that tests whether the number is *right*, not whether it *agrees* |

Constraints on S-C: CloverProof is software only (CLAUDE.md rule 1). We design and record trials; a farmer or research partner runs them. Yield-monitor file import, weather and satellite are out of scope today (CLAUDE.md, Stack); a yield record typed by the farmer is the in-scope minimum. Trial protocol and plot design are an agronomist/partner decision.

## 2. When ML is justified
**Pilot season 2027: no ML.** Arithmetic, not taste:

- **Mean Δ precision.** With SD σ known, n ≥ (1.96·σ/E)² for a 95 % half-width E; with σ unknown, iterate with t ([NIST e-Handbook 7.2.2.2](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc222.htm)). E = 0.5σ needs ≈ 16 (≈ 18 after t iteration); E = 0.25σ needs ≈ 62. Fields cluster in farms; design effect 1 + (m − 1)·ICC with m = 3 fields/farm and ICC = 0.5 (ESTIMATE; real ICC unknown) doubles that to ≈ 36 and ≈ 124 plans. The 2027 maximum of 10–40 fields on 5–10 farms gives a usable mean in **one** stratum at best.
- **Coefficients vs prediction.** Linear regression needs about 2 subjects per variable for unbiased coefficients and valid CIs (Austin & Steyerberg, J Clin Epidemiol 2015, [abstract](https://read.qxmd.com/read/25704724/the-number-of-subjects-per-variable-required-in-linear-regression-analyses)), but that is explanation, not prediction. The "10 events per variable" rule (Peduzzi 1996) applies to binary outcomes and is contested ([Wikipedia summary, secondary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_in_ten_rule)). For a prediction model of a continuous outcome, Riley et al. set four criteria including shrinkage ≤ 10 % ([BMJ 2020;368:m441](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.m441); implemented in [pmsampsize](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/pmsampsize/refman/pmsampsize.html)). Shrinkage criterion n = p / ((S − 1)·ln(1 − R²/S)), S = 0.9 — **our arithmetic, verify with pmsampsize**: p = 10 parameters, R² = 0.3 → ≈ 250 plans; R² = 0.2 → ≈ 400; p = 25, R² = 0.2 → ≈ 1,000. Inflate for farm clustering.
- **Threshold (ESTIMATE):** a delta-prediction model is reasonable at **≥ 400 agronomist-approved plans from ≥ 100 farms over ≥ 2 seasons, ≥ 2 human reviewers, plus one held-out season** for prospective validation. Method: Riley criterion at p = 10, R² = 0.2, before clustering. Volume path (ESTIMATE, not a forecast): 2027 ≤ 40 plans; 2028 ≈ 50 farms × 3 fields ≈ 150; 2029 ≈ 150 farms × 3 ≈ 450 cumulative ~640. So the earliest ML go/no-go is the **2029 season retrospective**, and only if growth happens.
- **What ML would be for:** (a) **delta prediction as a reviewer aid** — flag plans the agronomist is likely to edit and why; never changes a number, never bypasses the review gate (rule 8), never edits a pack; (b) **lab-report extraction** — draft-only, human-checked; **out of scope until CLAUDE.md allows OCR/LLM reading** (CLAUDE.md, Stack; open-data-backlog.md Docling row needs ~30 consented lab reports first); (c) later, response-curve models from S-C trials. A single-rater model learns that rater's style, not agronomy; say so in every ML memo.

**Project sketches** (all need a named human agronomist first):
1. **Rater agreement on spring wheat N** — Q: how far do 2–3 independent Ontario agronomists differ from each other and from the engine on the same blind cases? Data: 30–50 de-identified cases, blind. Partner: U of G faculty + 1 student. Programme: Mitacs (1–2 units). Timeline: apply Q2 2027, run summer–fall 2027. Cheapest; directly tests the D4 single-rater weakness.
2. **On-farm N-rate strips, Ontario winter wheat** — Q: does the validated pack's rate sit near the crop-response optimum on pilot fields? Data: replicated strips + harvest yield on 5–15 fields (ESTIMATE). Partner: Ridgetown / OSCIA-style on-farm network. Programme: Mitacs, later GFO (via the researcher). Timeline: design winter 2027–28, strips spring 2028, report fall 2028.
3. **Confidence-rule calibration** — Q: do high/medium/low labels track observed |Δ| and, later, response error? Data: S-A/S-B dataset across 2027–2028. Partner: statistics/data-science faculty. Programme: Mitacs; SR&ED records. Timeline: 2028.
4. **Manitoba pack transfer** — Q: what share of Ontario evidence transfers to a Manitoba spring wheat pack without refitting? Data: Manitoba guide methods + backup-region cases. Partner: U of Manitoba Soil Science. Programme: Mitacs; NSERC Alliance once ≥ 2 FTE. Timeline: 2029.
5. **Lab-report extraction accuracy** — only after CLAUDE.md permits OCR/LLM; Docling spike per open-data-backlog.md. Programme: IRAP (if eligible). Timeline: not before Phase 4.

**Strongest counter-argument:** R&D partnerships cost founder time the pilot needs, and a sole-founder company fails most cost-share gates; the right 2027 R&D is simply running the calibration loop well. Accepted for R1; partner work starts only after the pilot (R2).
