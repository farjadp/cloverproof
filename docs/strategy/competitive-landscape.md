## 1. Profiles

**FBN (Farmers Business Network)** - Private US company, High River AB Canadian base; sells inputs (crop protection, bulk fertilizer, Brandon MB centre Oct 2025), so not neutral ([Seed World](https://www.seedworld.com/canada/2018/04/23/silicon-valley-just-entered-the-seed-realm/), [BusinessWire 2025-10-20](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20251020391783/en/FBN-Invests-in-Manitoba-with-New-Brandon-Distribution-Centre), SNIPPET). Fertilizer recs: **Norm**, a chat advisor built on ChatGPT (2023), free to members, described as a first-line advisor, not a replacement for professionals ([AgFunderNews](https://agfundernews.com/why-farmers-business-network-launched-norm-an-ai-advisor-for-farmers-built-on-chatgpt), SNIPPET). Canadian tailoring of Norm: UNKNOWN. FBN Acre Plans (2024) is a crop-protection planning tool, not fertilizer ([BusinessWire 2024-01-24](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240124110165/en/FBN-Expands-Portfolio-for-Canadian-Growers-with-10-New-Crop-Protection-Products-Digital-Tools-and-Enhanced-Distribution-Network), SNIPPET). Membership reported as C$800/yr in 2018 (old; [Precision Farming Dealer](https://www.precisionfarmingdealer.com/articles/3365-farmers-business-network-expands-into-canada-acquires-yorkton-distributor), SNIPPET). Ontario presence: UNKNOWN (Prairie-centred evidence only). Records/audit/multi-farm: not found.

**Farmers Edge / Corvian** - Private since March 2024, majority-owned by Fairfax Financial ([Producer](https://www.producer.com/news/farmers-edge-returns-to-private-ownership), SNIPPET). Dec 2025: launched **Corvian**, an enterprise division (white-label platforms, data licensing, advisory); grower-facing Farmers Edge and Farmers Edge Laboratories (soil) continue ([BusinessWire 2025-12-10](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251210543212/en/Farmers-Edge-Announces-Corvian-A-New-Enterprise-Technology-Division-Built-to-Accelerate-Digital-Transformation-Across-Agriculture-and-Sustainable-Supply-Chains), SNIPPET). farmersedge.ca now redirects to [corvian.com](https://corvian.com/) (FACT), whose page lists Agronomy ("precision agronomy and nutrient management"), Soil Testing, Smart Reporting, claims intelligence; FarmCommand not mentioned (FACT). Own marketing claims VR and flat-rate prescriptions ([farmersedge.ca](https://farmersedge.ca/?p=86687), SNIPPET); who sets rates (company agronomist vs grower vs retailer): UNKNOWN. Pricing: none public; reported "customizable" structure (2024 sponsored, SNIPPET). Not vendor-neutral in the input sense (sells to retailers/enterprise; ESTIMATE). Shift to enterprise/B2B is a signal it is leaving the small grower (ESTIMATE).

**Climate FieldView (Bayer, ESTIMATE)** - Owner: Monsanto-origin ([Feedstuffs](https://www.feedstuffs.com/agribusiness-news/climate-corp-expands-platform-into-western-canada), SNIPPET); Bayer parentage is background knowledge, not confirmed this run. Eastern Canada since 2016 ([Farms.com](https://farms.com/ag-industry-news/climate-corporation-introduces-climate-fieldview-to-eastern-canada-820.aspx), SNIPPET). Price page: Trial $0, FieldView "starts at $499/year", **no currency stated**; Manual and Enhanced Scripting in paid tier; "Account Sharing" across devices; no nutrient-planning module named ([climate.com/en-ca/pricing](https://climate.com/en-ca/pricing), FACT). A Nitrogen Advisor exists via WinField (corn context); no evidence of Ontario winter wheat validation ([WinField](https://www.winfieldunited.com/news-and-insights/climate-fieldview-pro), SNIPPET). Rates are scripted by the user or a dealer; logging from machine data.

**Granular (Corteva)** - Corteva Canada still lists **Granular Insights** (plan, manage, analyze with "your trusted Corteva team"; [corteva.com/ca-en](https://www.corteva.com/ca-en/products-and-solutions/software-solutions-and-digital-services/granular-insights.html), SNIPPET); eastern Canada launch 2021, free sign-up per Corteva release (SNIPPET). ## 3. Gaps CloverProof fills (evidence-backed)

1. **No evidence of a per-number confidence level or immutable, hash-verified record in any product above.** Nothing read shows either; AgriSuite and Soil Test Manager emit calculations or a PDF with no change trail found. Scope caveat: Nutrien, Farmers Edge, TELUS pages are thin, so absence there is weaker.
2. **Neutral decision in the advisor workflow is empty.** The only multi-farm advisor tools found are Agworld (imports someone else's prescriptions), TELUS/Nutrien (retailer-owned) and Trimble (zones/prescriptions). None shows its own reasoned rate with a reason.
3. **Wheat N has no public calculator** (FCN 2025-04-15: table only), while free tools for P/K already exist. Our wheat-N-first launch avoids the crowded P/K lane (but see section 4).
4. **Retail/seed/input-owned platforms dominate** (FBN, Nutrien, Corteva, Bayer, TELUS-retail). Only OMAFRA/OSCIA, AgExpert (lender, ?) and Agworld (Semios) show no input sales in what I read (ESTIMATE).
5. Gap size is untested: no evidence yet that growers or advisors value a record of why (S3 hypotheses H1-H5 stay open).

## 4. Where they beat us

- **OSCIA Soil Test Manager and AgriSuite:** free, built on OMAFRA tables, already deliver P/K/N recommendations and a PDF; the 4R scoring calculator is a trust anchor. P/K from a test is the same job as theirs, with an official basis we lack. This is the strongest incumbent and was missing from S3.
- **Nutrien/retail:** human agronomist, field visit, prescriptions, as-applied data, credit and delivery, 500,000+ soil samples/yr (S3).
- **FieldView, Deere, Agworld, AgExpert:** machine-data logging, installed base, grower-controlled sharing, AgExpert lender-owned; free Deere account.
- **Agworld:** already has agronomist-grower collaboration, approvals of imported prescriptions, elemental plan reports in Canada.
- **Human accountability:** every retail/CCA alternative has a named person; our reviewer is an AI agent and 4R sign-off is out of reach until a human is named.

## 5. Threat moves (who could copy the wedge fastest; ESTIMATE)

1. **OSCIA / OMAFRA (fastest, low cost to them):** adding a saved history, confidence note or wheat N calculator to a free app already in use. No evidence they plan it.
2. **Agworld:** has the advisor multi-farm base, approvals and nutrient reports; adding a recommendation engine or per-number confidence is plausible. Evidence of intent: none.
3. **FBN Norm:** a ChatGPT-based advisor can produce a rate answer today; it lacks records and neutrality, but price is free to members. Threat to the grower-facing message, not to the advisor workflow.
4. **Nutrien/TELUS:** an audit-trail feature is within reach for them; their conflict of interest stays.
5. Slow: FieldView, Deere, Trimble, Granular, AgExpert are record/data pipes; a nutrient decision product is off their main path (ESTIMATE).
