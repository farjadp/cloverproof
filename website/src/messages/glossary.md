## Rules for translators
- Natural, not literal. Same meaning, no added claims, no softening or strengthening.
- No agronomic numbers, yields, savings or prices. Keep every "—" placeholder exactly as in English.
- Never say a third party (government, lab, buyer, certifier) accepts or approves a CloverProof document.
- "No soil test = nitrogen only, at most medium confidence" must stay exactly that strict.
- Privacy notice and consent checkbox: translate faithfully, sentence by sentence; do not add or drop rights or purposes.
- Keep `{placeholders}` (e.g. `{current}`, `{total}`) unchanged.
